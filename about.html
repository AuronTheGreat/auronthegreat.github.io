AIOS

Our first product launches February 10th, 2026. AIOS (AI Operating System) is a natural language interface for Linux that lets you talk to your computer like a person.

Want to install Docker? Just say "install Docker." Need to find large files eating up disk space? Ask "what's taking up space on my drive?" Want to set up a cron job? Tell it "remind me to backup my photos every Sunday."

No more memorizing commands. No more searching Stack Overflow for the right flags. Just natural conversation that gets things done.

It's built for developers, system administrators, and anyone who wants their computer to understand intent, not just syntax.
